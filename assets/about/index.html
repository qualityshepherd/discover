Discover is my public feed reader. A live-action blogroll curated by a human. These are the blogs I'm reading, currently. They're apt to change or come and go, just like a private feed reader.

Read here, or take a playlist or a single blog to your own reader. Every post is credited and links back to its blog. The words belong to whoever wrote them. No platforms, no accounts, no ads, no money.

Why public? The small web has two problems: finding blogs, and bloggers knowing anyone's reading. Discover helps with the first; Mentions help with the second.

Want your blog included? Suggest it. Rather not be in here? Email me and you're out.

Mentions

RSS mention feeds are created when a blog in discover links to another blog in discover. Someone thought enough of your post to write about it on their own site, using their name.

Discover doesn't own the relationships between bloggers. It observes relationships that already exist through hyperlinks.

Follow your mentions

Every site in discover has a feed of posts that linked to it. Drop it in your reader:

https://discover.brine.dev/api/mentions/yourdomain.com.xml

Show mentions with your posts

Add this Javascript snippet at the end of your post template or wherever your posts end. Mentions will display as a list or not at all if there are none:

<script src="https://discover.brine.dev/mentions-embed.js" defer></script>

Works on any blog that lets you edit a template: static, Bear, hand-written, whatever. No account, no tracking, no stylesheet. Mention api calls are not logged. They render as plain HTML.

Rather not run my script?

Fair. It's one small file. Grab it, read it, host it on your own site, and point it back here for the data. Then it can't change unless you change it:

<script src="/mentions-embed.js" data-api="https://discover.brine.dev" defer></script>

But I have a SPA

A <script> tag inserted via innerHTML never runs, so single-page sites call the embed themselves. Add data-manual to the tag, then pass it the post element:

<script src="https://discover.brine.dev/mentions-embed.js" data-manual defer></script>
// after rendering a post
const article = document.querySelector('article.post')
window.discoverMentions?.(article)

It reads the current URL by default. Pass the post's URL as a second argument if needed.

Running your own index?

Discover publishes every link its blogs made lately as one JSON file, and you can publish yours the same way. Read the spec.
